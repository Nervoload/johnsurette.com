brain-points.v1.bin — whole-brain surface point sample used by the "Scales of mind" scene.

Derived from the MNI ICBM152 2009a nonlinear symmetric grey- and white-matter probability maps
(as bundled in the nilearn package, nilearn/datasets/data/mni_icbm152_*_tal_nlin_sym_09a_converted.nii.gz)
by scripts/assets/build_brain_points.py: outer-surface voxels of the tissue mask, 20,000 points,
quantized, with estimated normals and approximate lobe labels (coordinate heuristics, not an atlas).

References:
  Fonov V, Evans AC, McKinstry RC, Almli CR, Collins DL. Unbiased nonlinear average age-appropriate
    brain templates from birth to adulthood. NeuroImage 2009;47:S102.
  Fonov V, Evans AC, Botteron K, Almli CR, McKinstry RC, Collins DL. Unbiased average age-appropriate
    atlases for pediatric studies. NeuroImage 2011;54(1):313-327.
  Collins DL, Zijdenbos AP, Baaré WFC, Evans AC. ANIMAL+INSECT: Improved Cortical Structure
    Segmentation. IPMI 1999.
